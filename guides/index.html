AQL inspection for apparel, explained

Final QC follows international quality standards (AQL), agreed with every client. Apparate accepts zero critical defects, AQL 2.5 for major defects and AQL 4.0 for minor defects.

Guide

Minimum order quantity for clothing manufacturing in Egypt

Our minimum order is 100 pieces per color. Final minimums are confirmed in a project-specific feasibility review, because materials, trims and construction can affect them. Capacity is up to 50,000 pieces per month.

Sampling and bulk production timelines for apparel

An initial sample takes about 5 working days and a pre-production sample about 3 working days. Standard bulk production takes about 4 weeks, and about 5 weeks in peak season. Bulk timing starts after pre-production approvals and materials are confirmed.

Garment printing techniques compared
