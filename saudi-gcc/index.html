Go global / Why Egypt

From Egypt, Go Global

Regional proximity can speed up sampling, feedback, approvals, and production follow-up. Egypt covers textiles, apparel, printing, embroidery, washing, finishing, trims, and specialist services in one country.

For regional clients

Production management and end-to-end quality oversight in Egypt, coordinated through FOB handover. Licensed partners support export documentation, customs, freight, and shipping.

For clients in Egypt

Production coordination through final local delivery, with quality control, approvals, clear progress updates, finishing, packing, and quantity confirmation.

Shipping

High-volume Saudi and GCC shipments are expected to move primarily by sea. Sokhna and Safaga are potential port options, subject to the confirmed route and freight partner. Route, responsibilities, shipping terms, lead-time and landed-cost potential are evaluated and confirmed for each order.
