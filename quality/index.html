Four critical quality gates

Quality built into the timeline

QC team experience: 10+ years.

01 · Before production

Sample inspection

The sample is checked against the pattern and the brief: measurements, materials, construction, trims, branding, and finishing.

02 · Start of the line

First-piece inspection

The first piece entering the production line is checked progressively at relevant stages to confirm the approved requirements transfer correctly.

03 · At 10–20% of production

Inline quality control

While production is running, work in progress is tested to detect and fix errors at the earliest stage: recurring defects, measurement variation, construction, and finishing.

04 · On receipt

Final quality inspection

The completed order is checked for workmanship, appearance, quantities, branding, finishing, and packing.

Quality assurance / Final QC

The standard stays visible

Final QC follows international quality standards (AQL), agreed with every client.

acceptance

Critical defects: Zero

Safety or regulatory failures, such as a stray needle or an unsafe sharp component.

acceptance level

Major defects: AQL 2.5

Defects that reduce usability, such as open seams, fabric holes, or broken stitches.

acceptance level

Minor defects: AQL 4.0

Small finishing defects that do not affect use, such as loose threads.

Supporting controls may include

  • Measurement, tolerances, fabric, shade and GSM checks
  • Shrinkage, colorfastness and composition verification
  • Branding placement and needle or metal detection where required
  • Corrective action and reinspection

Production acceptance is based on approved samples and documented inspections.

Questions brands ask us

Frequently asked questions

What quality standards do you inspect to?

Every order passes four quality gates: sample inspection, first-piece inspection, inline QC at 10–20% of production, and final inspection. Final QC follows AQL standards agreed with the client: zero acceptance for critical defects, AQL 2.5 for major defects and AQL 4.0 for minor defects.
